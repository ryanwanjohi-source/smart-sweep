Pay an Invoice

Monday, 23 November 2026

1. Find the invoice

2. Choose how to pay

     

M-Pesa phone number

You will get an M-Pesa prompt on this phone — enter your PIN to pay.

Pay on your phone with Lipa na M-Pesa → PayBill. When you continue, the API gives you the business number — your account number is your invoice code. The payment completes when Safaricom confirms it.

When you continue, the API gives you the Smart Sweep bank account and a reference to quote on the transfer. The Accountant confirms the payment once the transfer lands.

Pay at the Smart Sweep office. The payment is recorded as received as soon as you continue.

Result

Payments are handled by the Smart Sweep Payments API (api/payments.php), which accepts M-Pesa, M-Pesa PayBill, Bank Transfer and Cash.
