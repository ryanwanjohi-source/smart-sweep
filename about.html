About Smart Sweep

Monday, 9 November 2026

What is Smart Sweep?

Smart Sweep is a smart garbage management system for urban estates in Kenya. It uses bins fitted with sensors to measure how full each bin is, then plans collection routes so that trucks only travel to bins that actually need emptying.


The system brings residents, dispatchers, collection drivers and accountants together in one place: residents request collections and pay for the service, dispatchers watch the bins and plan the routes, drivers follow the routes and update their job status, and accountants handle invoices and payments.

The Problem It Solves

In many estates, garbage trucks follow a fixed timetable. They drive to bins that are almost empty, wasting fuel and time — and at the same time, other bins overflow before the truck comes, leaving waste in the open. There is no easy way to know which bin needs attention, and residents have no simple way to request a collection or follow up a payment.


Smart Sweep fixes this by letting the bins speak for themselves: every bin reports its own fill level, so collections happen when they are needed, not just when the timetable says so.

How It Works

Step 1 — The bin measures itself. An ultrasonic sensor measures the fill level and a load cell measures the weight. The readings are sent to the system over MQTT.


Step 2 — An alert is raised. When a bin reaches 80% full, the system raises a fill-level alert for the dispatcher.


Step 3 — A route is planned. The dispatcher schedules a collection and the system works out an efficient route covering the bins that need emptying.


Step 4 — The driver collects. The collection driver follows the route, empties the bins and updates the job status. If something goes wrong, the driver reports a delay.


Step 5 — The resident pays. The accountant generates the invoice and the resident pays, for example through M-Pesa. Every action is recorded in the system's audit log.
